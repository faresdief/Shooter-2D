FREE BROWSER GAME

Shooter-2D

Survive waves of enemies in a top-down arena. Earn coins, upgrade your weapons and take on bosses. Play Story, Arcade, Endless or the Daily Challenge right in your browser.

Keyboard and mouse

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Aim with the mouse and hold left click to shoot.
  • Hold a direction and press Shift to dash. Press E or Space to use Nova.
  • Press P or Escape to pause.

Phone and tablet

  • Turn your phone sideways to play.
  • Drag on the left to move; drag on the right to aim and shoot.
  • Tap Dash while moving. If needed, choose Phone under Controls in Settings.
